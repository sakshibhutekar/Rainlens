About RainLens

IFS is ECMWF’s physics based Integrated Forecasting System. AIFS is ECMWF’s AI based forecasting system. Rainfall is six-hourly accumulation derived from ECMWF Open Data.

Data: ECMWF Open Data, CC-BY-4.0

Model output is not an official warning. Known limitations: 0.25 degree grid, six-hourly intervals, and no verification against observations.
